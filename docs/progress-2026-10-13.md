## Product decisions

Alerts require a separate opt-in: creating an account or following an organisation does not subscribe a user. Paid coaching is identified separately from academy recruitment trials. Before sending, known listings are checked against official availability evidence; changed or unavailable evidence holds delivery. Ambiguous mail handoffs are held for review rather than blindly retried.

The public case study contains narrative and scoped validation results. Application source, research registers, database migrations, deployment configuration, raw logs, credentials, personal recipient information and unsubscribe capabilities remain private.

## Step 4 evidence

Account data export, permanent account deletion and saved-choice loading were implemented and tested in the protected development preview.

- The expanded JavaScript suite passed 118 tests; the final preview build passed compilation and TypeScript checks.
- A live export included all 501 disposable test notes, account selections and alert history. It contained only the authenticated account’s records, with credentials and unsubscribe capabilities excluded.
- Anonymous requests, cross-origin deletion and missing deletion confirmation were rejected. Deletion requires an explicit typed confirmation and a recent sign-in.
- Deleting the first disposable account revoked its refresh session and removed its active account data. Its old token could no longer export data, while the second test account remained intact.
- Both disposable accounts were deleted after validation. Database checks found zero remaining fixture accounts, sessions or application records. No real user account was deleted.
- The browser export control worked. Previously saved opportunity choices survived a reload, with marketing remaining off.
- Saved-record loading now paginates results, rejects partial failures, cancels stale requests and offers retry after a timeout. Results belonging to a previous account are hidden after an identity change.

The export covers active application records rather than supplier-held mailbox, log or backup records. Concurrent edits can appear at different times across exported tables. Account deletion does not recall sent emails or delete downloaded copies, support correspondence or supplier backups. These limits are explained in the preview.

## Step 5 evidence

The exercised release checks passed in the protected development preview, with one provider security advisory retained.

- The JavaScript suite passed 118 tests, and the final preview build passed compilation and TypeScript checks.
- At a 390px browser viewport, the explorer, opportunities and account screens had no horizontal document overflow. Buttons and map controls were adjusted to 44px targets. This was a browser viewport check, not a physical-device or complete accessibility audit; 320px validation was not established.
- Anonymous discovery pages returned successfully. Private export, alert, journey and deletion endpoints rejected requests without a PitchReach session. QA bypassed the hosting protection layer only; it did not bypass application authentication.
- Account-isolation and persistent routing-budget database checks passed with disposable fixtures rolled back. Private responses were not cached. Framing and content-type protection headers were verified.
- A fresh branded sign-in email arrived, and the user confirmed opening the link and signing in. Opportunity marketing remained off. Inbox versus spam placement was not established.
- A live Slough-area-to-Reading training-venue comparison returned 35 minutes by car and 110 minutes by bus. The interface showed the programme-allocation uncertainty. Switching transport reused the result.
- Routing limits now persist across server instances: six comparisons per minute and twenty per day per account, with a shared daily cap of one hundred. Database failure blocks a new provider request.
- At that shared cap, a 31-day month permits at most 3,100 car and 3,100 bus requests. Based on current request parameters and Google's published rates, the estimate before free allowances is $46.50; a conservative assumption that both modes use the Pro rate gives $62. These are request-based estimates, not an invoice, and exclude other services, tax and currency conversion. Free allowances are shared across the billing account. [Google pricing](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/pricing), [SKU rules](https://developers.google.com/maps/billing-and-pricing/sku-details).

Supabase still reports disabled leaked-password protection. The application currently presents magic-link sign-in, and the provider makes that protection a paid-plan feature. No account credentials or paid plan were changed. This checkpoint does not claim an entirely clean security audit. [Supabase password security](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/password-security).

Anonymous browsing remains available in the preview. The existing adult account eligibility rule was not changed; younger-user account design requires a separate product decision.
